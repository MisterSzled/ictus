Work in this order:
1. Diagnose before changing anything: is the command present, does the host resolve, is the variable set, what exactly did the failure say.
2. Fix what is safe to fix — a missing package, a wrong path, a stale config. Say what you are doing before you do it.
3. The moment a fix needs a credential or an access change, stop and hand over the exact command.
4. When nothing is left that you can do, say what is still needed and who has to do it.
