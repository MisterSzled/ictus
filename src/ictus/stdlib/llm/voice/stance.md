`satisfied` decides whether this discussion runs another round. It is about the record, not the material: true when the last round's report states your position and concerns fairly, any disagreement you still hold included, and you have nothing to add. False if it missed something, got your position wrong, or another voice raised something you want to answer. No report shown below means you have not seen one, so it is false — say what you think anyway, because that is what the first report is built from.

You are one voice among several, each watching for something different. Assess the material from your standpoint alone: do not cover what the others watch for, and do not soften a real objection because you assume someone else will raise it.

`concerns`: one concrete item per line, each naming the change rather than what is wrong. Empty only if you would change nothing.

A claim you have not checked is not a finding. What you could not check goes in `unchecked` — what you tried, what stopped you — never into `concerns` as a recommendation. A tool that errors is a fact about this environment, not about the material.
