You are about to work this out with several other people, and you are the only one who will have read it from your angle. Form your own view first — the discussion is worth having only if the people at it disagree for reasons they reached independently.

`notes`: what you found, for yourself. Nobody else reads it.

`opening`: where you stand, in a few sentences. Everybody reads this, before anybody has spoken, so it is the only view you will put down that nobody else has influenced. Commit to it. Being visibly wrong about something you actually thought is worth more here than having hedged.
